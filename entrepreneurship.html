Phasing down Promise Kept Company

Grew Promise Kept Company to $300k in revenue. My philosophy on what I want out of my career has changed since I started, and I've made the difficult decision to phase down the company to pursue a new challenge.

The self-driven MBA

What I learned

At the time that I started these endeavors, I subscribed to a bootstrapped, not-sexy style of entrepreneurship that prioritized speed to being a profitable company (on the order of $500k–$3M ARR) as opposed to the Silicon Valley, exponential-growth approach to startups. I pursued ideas I thought had a need, quickly made prototypes and tested demand, and then iterated from there. I started three projects at the same time, and one, Promise Kept Company, endured and grew to $300k in revenue. Since launching out on this adventure, which I now view as a self-driven MBA, my philosophy on what I want out of my career has changed, and I've made the difficult decision to phase down Promise Kept Company.
